## LinkedIn article draft

### A preflight check for publishing the same announcement on Facebook and Instagram

When an announcement goes to two platforms, check each destination before assuming both are ready. The same caption may work in both feeds, but the linked accounts and media can still behave differently.

**Check the accounts.** Confirm the Facebook Page and Instagram profile are the ones your team expects to use. If the profile is missing from your scheduler, check the Page link in Meta before reconnecting.

**Write a caption that stands alone.** Start with the announcement’s date, location or main change. A caption such as “Saturday’s pottery workshop starts at 11 a.m. in our Sector 8 studio” carries the useful details even if a preview gets cropped. Choose a photo or supported video that makes sense alongside that caption, and open any linked media in a signed-out browser to check access.

**Verify the destination after the scheduled time.** Confirm delivery on each account. If the scheduler cannot tell whether one platform received the post, check that platform before retrying. A second publish can turn a temporary status problem into a duplicate announcement.

I’m building Postzyo for this workflow. Its Scheduler shows each destination separately so a team can see which post still needs attention. [Postzyo](https://postzyo.com/)
