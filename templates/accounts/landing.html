Which Facebook and Instagram accounts can I connect?

You can connect Facebook Pages you manage and their linked Instagram Business accounts through Meta.

Does Postzyo publish scheduled posts automatically?

Yes. The Scheduler sends due posts and shows a result for each destination. Temporary delays can retry automatically. If a delivery needs verification, the queue flags it so you can check the destination in Meta.

Can I schedule videos and Reels?

Yes. The Scheduler supports Facebook videos and Instagram Reels. Use supported media and check platform requirements when preparing your post.

What does AI Insights use?

Reports use available snapshots and recent post performance from your connected account to suggest ideas and posting strategies.
